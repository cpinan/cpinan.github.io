Stat Calculator

A Pokémon stat calculator for Android. Pick a Pokémon, set IVs, EVs, nature, level and generation — see the final stats.

What it does

  • All nine generations. Gen I and II use DVs and Stat Exp; Gen III onward use IVs, EVs and nature multipliers. Switching generation re-clamps the spread.
  • All 1,025 Pokémon, searchable offline, with base stats and types from PokéAPI cached for offline use.
  • Compare builds side by side, with per-stat winners.
  • Save up to 20 builds and reload them into the calculator or the comparator.
  • A formula reference sheet, so the arithmetic is inspectable rather than magic.
  • Six languages — English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian.
  • Works offline once a Pokémon has been looked up.

Privacy

No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics. Your builds stay on your device. The app is ad-supported, and the privacy policy sets out exactly what the ad SDK collects and what it does not — including the Android backup behaviour that most policies quietly omit.
