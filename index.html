Corta Spam

Open-source call blocker for Peru and beyond. Rule engine with wildcard patterns, country matching, quiet hours, repeat-call rules and a bundled offline spam list. It does not declare the internet permission, so it cannot phone home.

Atari's Tempest (1981) rebuilt for Android in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. The vector tube is drawn on a Compose Canvas, state runs through a pure MVI reducer, and the whole thing is documented as a 19-chapter tutorial.

  • Compose Canvas
  • MVI
